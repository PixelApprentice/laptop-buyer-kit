About Laptop Buyer Kit

            
Laptop Buyer Kit is an offline-first, open-source laptop diagnostics platform built for used hardware buyers, refurbishers, and technicians.

            
            
Core Engineering Principles

            
    
              
  • Offline by Default: Zero telemetry, zero uploads, zero remote script tracking. Everything evaluates on your machine.
    • 
              
  • Transparent Uncertainty: Missing or unsupported metrics are never converted into a fake "100% Pass".
    • 
              
  • Discrepancy Investigation: Non-destructive discrepancy detection without automatic accusations of fraud.
    • 
              
  • Safe Buyer Mode: Read-only inspection; no destructive wipes, partition edits, or unapproved firmware modifications.
    •
